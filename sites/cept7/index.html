Why we decided to present this

Every day on Yuehai Campus starts with the same five-second gamble between three very different commutes.

Daily Friction

Do you usually suffer from campus commuting?

Each mode looks tempting until the bell rings at 8:20 AM or 1:50 PM.

Walking

“Thousands” of people

Long cross-campus paths take 15–20 minutes on foot, climbing stairs in Shenzhen heat while weaving through class crowds.

Cycling

Traffic jam on paths?

Pedestrian alleys choke bicycles at peak class hours, steep campus hills sap momentum, and parking racks overflow.

Campus Shuttle

(Almost) infinite queue

Waiting for a loop bus that arrives full — and then still walking 5–10 minutes from the stop to your building.

III
Part III · Results & Suggestions

What should we choose?

Weighing every mode’s real pros and cons, then turning the numbers into three effortless daily habits.

Balanced Verdict

Every mode has a clear strength — and a clear trap

Walk

4.5 km/h
Pros
  • Zero waiting, zero parking, zero fare.
  • Immune to crowds and slopes (<3% uphill change).
  • Direct stair shortcuts bikes and buses cannot use.
Cons
  • 2.7× slower than cycling across campus.
  • Misses 60% of 10-min cross-building class breaks.
  • Tiring in summer heat or rain past 800 m.
Best for: Trips under 500 m & stair-heavy shortcuts.

Cycle

15.8 km/h
Pros
  • Fastest on 100% of 380 campus routes (median 4.1m).
  • Reaches all 30 teaching pairs within a 10-min break.
  • Main roads stay smooth even during class peaks.
Cons
  • −25% slower on crowded pedestrian paths at peak.
  • Northbound climbs cut speed down to ~12 km/h.
  • Shared-bike hunt, rack parking, and rain exposure.
Best for: Any rush >500 m & tight class transfers.

Shuttle

12–17 km/h
Pros
  • Effortless, sheltered from rain and midday sun.
  • Saves 5–6 min over walking on long ring-side routes.
  • Ideal with luggage, heavy bags, or after sports.
Cons
  • Stop walking + ~4.1m wait eat 85% of total trip time.
  • Beats walking on only 12% of pairs; never beats bikes.
  • Full buses and fewer Sunday vehicles stretch waits.
Best for: >1.2 km ring-to-ring trips or bad weather.
Practical Suggestions

Three simple rules — plus a live campus route advisor

R1
Under 500 m (≤ 6 min walk)? Just walk.
Unlocking and parking a shared bike eats 1–2 min, wiping out the speed edge on short hops like South Dorms → Zhizhen.
R2
Tight break or cross-campus? Cycle on main roads.
Bypass pedestrian alleys during 10-min peaks to avoid the −25% crowd slowdown, and budget +1 extra minute when climbing north.
R3
Take the shuttle only if both stops are ≤ 3 min away.
Check live bus location first: if your wait exceeds 4–5 min on a mid-length route, walking beats waiting.
Tip
For campus planners
South Dorms & Zhili sit 7–10 min from shuttle stops — adding a South Campus feeder link and peak-hour bike lanes would unlock the biggest gains.
